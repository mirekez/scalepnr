# unbound_completion_frontier

Captured unbound completion.

- Load the recorded free numeric alternatives with abstract node names.
- Keep the source and requested sink at their captured coordinates.
- Install a real foreign owner on an alternative destination entry.
- Preserve the owner route throughout the requesting search.
- Immediate cancellation must publish no route or lease.
- Try the production source budgets in their normal order.
- A failed bounded attempt must leave route registrations unchanged.
- It must also release its own occupied nodes on return.
- Persistent proved deadends are allowed to remain.
- The recorded free completion must eventually reach the requested pin.
- No preemption is permitted for that free connection.
- Require one binding and one complete returned route.
- Check source and sink identity in the resulting fragments.
- Audit final ownership, then remove only the requesting route.
- The foreign landing must stay leased after requester teardown.
- Finally remove the foreign fixture route and audit again.

- Updated the existing case for recursive routing.
- Preserve immediate deadends independently of route leases.

Implementation:
