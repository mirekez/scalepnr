# Free detour outside the forward-search frontier

Example 1 run 6 failed signal 165576, from register 6324/Q at resource
`(65,21)/132` to cell 17492/I3 at `(14,12)/135`. Its CB endpoints were
`(66,21)/LOCAL142` and `(15,12)/LOCAL106`.

The 291 captured frontier audits covering 288 distinct Tiles, and the thirteen
final sink-neighbourhood audits, reported no orphan leases, missing leases or
registrations, stale registrations or conflicting drivers. The free search
allocated 32,768 states, including 22,279 distinct physical nodes, expanded
30,317 states and rejected 3,218 insertions at its budget. Only four admissions
repeated the same node/cost/hop signature; most replacements were distinct
improvements, not a simple duplicate-state loop.

Its closest reached Tile was `(25,8)`, ten Tiles from the sink in Chebyshev
distance. Grounding had zero starting states. A check against the real loaded
topology also found no reached SRC whose physical jump entered the five-Tile
Grounding window; this was not a skipped boundary-entry candidate.

The independent input-preserving search found a fully free, 41-node, 19-hop
path after 1,383,316 states, without exhausting its diagnostic budget. The
forward search reached `(70,20)/SRC818`, but could not allocate its landing
`(75,24)/DST818`. The useful detour initially moves farther from the sink.

`basic_detour_frontier.txt` contains all 62,376 unique admitted arcs from the
failed free search plus 34 missing witness arcs. Each row is
`x y kind id next_x next_y next_kind next_id`, using LOCAL=0, JOINT=1,
SRC=3 and DST=4. `basic_detour_path.txt` preserves the independent witness.
This is the recorded admitted frontier, not the complete device graph;
unexpanded branches can have further continuations outside this fixture.
All textual node names are abstract, resource slots are normalized, and
unavailable foreign-owner alternatives are reduced to live occupied outlets.
Every recorded LOCAL/JOINT passed the real structural-productivity check.
Simply deleting its congested outgoing branches made the initial fixture
falsely classify some of these nodes as terminal; that weaker fixture passed
and did not reproduce the failure. Each fixture Tile now has a separately
routed foreign-owner net with two additional cells and a private occupied
outlet. Recorded LOCAL/JOINT nodes can see that outlet structurally, but cannot
enter it. This preserves the observed structural/free-search distinction
without creating a free bypass or unowned occupancy flags.

`basic_detour_sources.txt` additionally records 8,983 raw SRC mappings and
whether each SRC has a JOINT continuation. All captured free jump arcs match
this metadata from the loaded device. Omitting busy/unexpanded landings changed
811 SRC priorities, so the final fixture preserves their physical coordinates.
Unobserved landings use owned blocked DSTs; known-free landings keep their real
node identity. Private obstruction routes are generated by the actual router,
and their SRC, DST, JOINT and input leases are independently audited.

The test uses the normal 32,768-state/200-hop limits, fixed endpoints and an
unchanged routing picture. A larger-budget control and a disconnected-input
control distinguish budget exhaustion from a missing terminal connection.
Every case also checks read-only reachability, cancellation, route ownership
and teardown. No cell relocation or generated-route assistance is involved.

On unchanged routing code, the strengthened normal-budget case failed with
numeric budget exhaustion; both the larger-budget and disconnected controls
passed. These results are in `/tmp/detour-red4.log` (39.52 seconds for all three).

Reducing the free-search Manhattan weight from two to one passes all three
cases without changing their limits or placement. An initial version completed
in 22,851 allocated states; the final version also preserves the raw SRC
priority metadata and passes. A separately compiled weight-two reference fails
the final normal-budget case with 32,768 allocated states and 22,320 distinct
nodes, while passing its larger-budget and disconnected controls. The final
weight-one version finds the witnessed 19-hop free path in about 450 ms of
debug-build search time. The full fixture takes longer because it audits every
live obstruction as well as the test net.
All sixteen earlier Basic-budget/source-priority cases also pass, including
the previous 62-hop LOCAL-network regression. The full suite passed 184/184
tests in 2,366.34 seconds, including all eleven puzzles. The final SRC-metadata
fixture was also rerun directly in all three modes and against the old-weight
reference after its extra fidelity checks were added. Real Example 1 validation
remains a separate required check.

The subsequent normal-limit replay completed runs 1–5 (run 5 routing took
357.714 seconds), then failed run 6 on signal 166890 with a different
placement/routing snapshot. A targeted replay reproduced that connection
failure from `(41,8)/LOCAL146` to `(37,36)/LOCAL83`. Its bounded free search
allocated 32,768 states and stopped six Tiles away in Chebyshev distance.
All 2,140 recorded frontier ownership audits were clean. An independent
input-preserving search exhausted 2,929,083 reachable states without finding
a fully free path, without hitting its diagnostic limit. Checking the loaded
topology also found no reached outside-window SRC jumping into Grounding.
Whether a complete path exists using permitted Takeoff/Grounding preemption
is unresolved. These results do not establish end-to-end Example 1 success,
and the earlier signal-166890 regression is a different captured snapshot.

`fpga.basic_detour_audit` separately tests the failed-task diagnostic. Its
normal routing budget is deliberately one state, while an explicit read-only
65,536-state probe discovers the same 41-node path. The task must still return
failure, create no binding or wire vector, never request a committed cut, and
leave all foreign-owner audits clean. Against the previous implementation it
fails because no diagnostic witness is reported; the new probe passes. This
is coverage for read-only diagnostics, not evidence that signal 166890 is
fixed or that a larger production budget should be allowed.
