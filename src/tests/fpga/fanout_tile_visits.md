# fanout_tile_visits

Angle-guided Fanouts may revisit a Tile.

- Start from an installed source-connected trunk and retained suffix.
- The retained path has already entered the same Tile twice.
- Offer a third arrival through a distinct free DST.
- That arrival leads to the mapped destination input.
- Use both near-target and more distant geometries.
- Also offer a separate detour in the alternative controls.
- Invoke production Fanouts with a small per-call recursion allowance.
- Resume until the route completes or the bounded attempt count ends.
- Generic angle tracing must not reject Tile repetition.
- Keep the chosen third-arrival SRC and DST leased.
- Require complete grounding at the requested sink.
- Do not apply the combinatorial Docking visit rule to this prefix.
- The existing trunk remains registered to its real owner.
- The completed suffix must retain coherent fragment ownership.
- Audit every Tile for orphan or missing leases.
- Check that all route registrations remain valid.

History:

- Revised the existing regression for recursive routing and immediate deadends.

Coverage:

- Uses production entry points and explicit ownership assertions.

Fixture connectivity and node names are vendor-agnostic.
