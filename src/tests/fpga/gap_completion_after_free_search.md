# gap_completion_after_free_search

Grounding completion after bounded free search.

- Retain a source prefix and a downstream GAP island.
- Keep a wide numeric component that does not freely reach the sink.
- Install a real foreign transit owner on the required final approach.
- Also expose a free terminal ingress on a disconnected island.
- The reverse index must report that free ingress honestly.
- It must not be mistaken for a source-connected route.
- Allow 1024 labels for each bounded free search.
- No recursive ancestor may continue after the cap.
- Attempt terminal preemption only after free exploration returns.
- Check that the failed searches left all real owners intact.
- Permit only the captured transit SRC to be cut.
- Validate its actual victim before applying the cut.
- Require exactly one cut and a complete three-hop replacement.
- Use one source search and no cached frontier reuse.
- Check expansion counts against the explicit allowances.
- Audit final fragments, leases, and registrations.
